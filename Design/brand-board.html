Naming & app icon

The working name PDFit is already taken on the App Store by a scanner with the same features. Below: three replacement names, four icon concepts shown at real iPhone sizes, and the pairings that work best.

Names

Taken

PDFit

“PDFit-AI PDF&Scanner” already exists: same features (scan, convert, merge), 4.8★ from 470 ratings. Shipping under this name risks rejection or a takedown, and it's hard to find in search.

RecommendedNo match found

Pagefold

Pages + folding: merge, split, compress, sign. It says “documents” immediately, is easy to say and spell in any accent, and the folded-corner icon comes straight from the name.

App Store title: Pagefold – Scan & PDF Tools (27/30)
No match found

Glassine

The name of a translucent paper, which matches the app's glass design. The most premium and distinctive option, but most people won't know the word, so the subtitle has to do more work.

App Store title: Glassine: Scan, Sign & PDF (27/30)
No match found

Sheaf

A bundle of papers. Short and elegant, and a natural fit for the stacked-sheets icon. Less familiar to non-native English speakers, and close to “sheet” in spoken search.

App Store title: Sheaf – Scanner & PDF Editor (29/30)

“No match found” means no app with that name showed up in an App Store web search on 18 Nov 2026. That is not a trademark clearance; check the trademark registry and the App Store Connect name check before committing.

Icon concepts

Each one is a full-bleed 1024 px SVG master (iOS adds the rounded corners). Small sizes are shown because the home screen is where icons are actually seen.

A · Fold

Page with a folded corner over a glass sheet, in the app's indigo-to-violet gradient. Pairs with Pagefold.

B · Scan frame

Viewfinder corners, a page and a scan beam. The clearest at 29 px and works with any name, but it stresses scanning over the other tools.

C · Stack

Three fanned sheets (merge, split, compress), from teal to indigo. Pairs with Sheaf.

D · Glassine

A frosted glass sheet over a page, with the text blurring behind it. A light icon that stands out on dark wallpapers. Pairs with Glassine.
